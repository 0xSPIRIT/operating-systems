# CPU Scheduling

Burst Time            - Total amount of CPU time a process needs to complete
Arrival Time          - When the process enters the ready queue
Completion Time       - When process finishes execution
Turnaround Time (TAT) - Total time from arrival to completion
Waiting Time (WT)     - Total time a process spends sitting in the ready queue
Gantt Chart           - Horizontal bar chart showing time.
